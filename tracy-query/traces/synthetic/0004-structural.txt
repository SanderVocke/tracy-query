TRACY-QUERY SYNTHETIC STRUCTURAL FIXTURE
========================================

File: 0004-structural.tracy
Tracy: 0.13.1
Source: ../../tests/fixtures/tracy_structural_fixture.cpp

Expected records
----------------

- GPU zones: 1 custom-context interval from 1,200 ns to 1,700 ns
- Thread context switches: 1 interval from 1,150 ns to 1,750 ns
- Per-CPU slices: 1 interval on CPU 0 from 1,150 ns to 1,750 ns
- Derived CPU-usage points: 3 after Tracy's load-time baseline reconstruction

The fixture begins with Tracy's public import constructor and injects the
otherwise platform-dependent structures through a test-only mutable accessor.
That accessor is available only when `TRACY_QUERY_FIXTURE_ACCESS` is explicitly
defined. The resulting capture is written by Tracy 0.13.1's own `Worker::Write`
and then read back normally by tracy-query. Production tracy-query builds do
not expose the mutable accessor.

This fixture is synthetic format coverage. Its timestamps and relationships
are intentionally simple and should not be interpreted as a real scheduler or
GPU workload.
